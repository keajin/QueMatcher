Display board

A separate, read-only window for your players. Organizer searches and dialogs stay on this laptop.

Shows up to 10 courts in two rows of five and the first 7 queued matches in one row. Courts rotate only above 10; available players page only when needed. You can move and resize the board window.

Switch configuration?

Today's attendance, the shared queue, active matches, timers, and match history will be cleared. All saved configurations, players, and courts will be kept.

Repeat their last match?

You can change the queued group or start this match anyway.
